🎙️ About Voice Studio

Voice Studio is an intelligent audio enhancement engine designed for creators, podcasters, voiceover artists, and remote workers. It automatically cleans background noises, balances uneven vocal volume, and trims awkward silence in seconds.

🧠 Neural Noise Isolation

Eliminates continuous fan noise, AC hums, room air hiss, and electrical buzz while keeping speech full and natural.

📈 Dynamic Speech Leveler

Lifts quiet whispered phrases and balances uneven microphone distances without raising background room noise.

🔇 Natural Silence Trimming

Cuts empty dead air and pauses without clipping words, making conversations crisp, engaging, and professional.

🔊 Broadcast Loudness (-16 LUFS)

Complies with international loudness standards (EBU R128) required by YouTube, Spotify, and podcast distributors.

⚡ How It Works
  1. Upload Your File: Select any audio or video recording up to 25 MB (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG).
  2. Automatic Pre-Decoding: The system automatically pre-decodes raw packets into uncompressed 48 kHz linear PCM, healing corrupt headers and bitrate inconsistencies.
  3. Single-Pass Processing: Our DSP pipeline cleans, articulates, levels, and masters your voice with zero robotic phasing.
  4. Instant Preview & Download: Compare before and after audio side by side, then download your studio-ready MP3 file.
🔒 Privacy First: Your recordings are processed locally or in isolated serverless memory. Files are permanently purged after 5 minutes and never shared or retained.
